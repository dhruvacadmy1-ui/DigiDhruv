## 🎮 About the Game

The **Missing Tables Game** is an interactive learning tool designed to help students master multiplication tables (5-16) through engagement and gamification.

### Game Features

#### 🌱 Three Difficulty Levels
| Level | Name | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| Level 1 | 🌱 Very Easy | Multiplication basics (tables 1-4) |
| Level 2 | 🌿 Medium | Intermediate practice (tables 5-8) |
| Level 3 | 🔥 Super | Advanced challenges (tables 9-12) |

#### ⭐ Core Capabilities
- ✅ **Instant Feedback** - Know immediately if your answer is correct
- 🎵 **Sound Effects** - Audio cues for engagement and feedback
- 🏆 **Leaderboard System** - Track your top 5 scores
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** - Play on desktop, tablet, or mobile
- ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight** - No external dependencies, pure web technologies
- 💾 **Score Persistence** - Scores saved locally in your browser

## 🎓 Learning Approach

### How It Works

1. **Practice Questions** - 5 questions per difficulty level (15 total)
2. **Instant Feedback** - Correct answers advance your progress
3. **Progressive Difficulty** - Master easier tables before harder ones
4. **Score Tracking** - See your improvement over time
5. **Leaderboard** - Friendly competition with yourself and others

### Why This Method Works

✨ **Spaced Repetition** - Regular practice reinforces memory
✨ **Immediate Feedback** - Know right away if you're correct
✨ **Gamification** - Scoring and leaderboards increase motivation
✨ **Progressive Complexity** - Build confidence before advancing
✨ **Bite-Sized Learning** - Short sessions fit into any schedule
